# Weekly Sourcing Note #39 — Payment terms that protect both sides

*2026-09-26 — by [SOLAIREVA](https://www.solaireva.com), an outdoor furniture manufacturer in Shunde, China*

One thing we told buyers this week about sourcing furniture from China:

- 30% deposit / 70% against Bill of Lading is the fair middle ground for wholesale furniture from China. Anyone demanding 100% up front is either new, desperate, or a trader with no factory to protect.

- A genuine factory negotiates because it wants repeat orders. A trader hedges because it has no skin in the game.
