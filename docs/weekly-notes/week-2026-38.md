# Weekly Sourcing Note #38 — OEM color matching, done right

*2026-09-19 — by SOLAIREVA, an outdoor furniture manufacturer in Shunde, China*

One thing we told buyers this week about sourcing furniture from China:

- A hotel buyer matched one cushion color across three orders over 18 months. The factory kept the fabric lot reference in its system so every reorder matched. Ask your supplier if they archive lot references — most don't.

- For custom outdoor furniture, approve the pre-production sample in writing before mass production. That sample is your color, size and quality contract.
