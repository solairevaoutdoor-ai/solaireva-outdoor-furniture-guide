# Weekly Sourcing Note #36 — Why the cheapest quote is rarely the cheapest

*2026-09-05 — by SOLAIREVA, an outdoor furniture manufacturer in Shunde, China*

One thing we told buyers this week about sourcing furniture from China:

- On a recent 40HQ comparison, the lowest quote was 18% under the average. The catch: 1.2mm frame instead of 1.5mm, and a UV additive package at half the standard dose. The container landed fine — the furniture faded in two seasons.

- Get the spec sheet written into the PO: frame wall thickness, coating, rattan type, foam density. The PO is your contract.
