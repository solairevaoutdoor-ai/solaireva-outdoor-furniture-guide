# Weekly Sourcing Note #35 — Lead time in peak season

*2026-08-28 — by SOLAIREVA, an outdoor furniture manufacturer in Shunde, China*

One thing we told buyers this week about sourcing furniture from China:

- Container space tightens from August as US retailers restock before Black Friday. If your first wholesale patio furniture order lands in Q4, book your freight early or expect 2-3 extra weeks.

- Ask a supplier for their current production lead time in writing — not 'about 30 days'. A factory that can't commit a ship week has a full or unreliable line.
