# Weekly Sourcing Note #37 — Mixed containers are the entry point

*2026-09-07 — by SOLAIREVA, an outdoor furniture manufacturer in Shunde, China*

One thing we told buyers this week about sourcing furniture from China:

- Most first-time buyers don't need a full container of one SKU. A mixed 40HQ with sofas, dining sets and umbrellas tests your market before you commit to per-SKU volumes. Real factories in Shunde do mixed loads as standard.

- Run tools/container-calculator.py with your carton sizes to plan the mix.
