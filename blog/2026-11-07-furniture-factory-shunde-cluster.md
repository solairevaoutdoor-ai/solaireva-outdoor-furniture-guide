# Furniture Factory Shunde: Why the Cluster Decides Your Order

## A cluster is a lead time advantage

Shunde and the surrounding Foshan area hold outdoor furniture's whole supply chain inside a short radius: powder coaters, weaving, foam, hardware, glass, packaging and the freight consolidators who fill containers.

That density shows up in small things. A colour batch can be redone in days instead of weeks. A replacement cover or a set of feet can be produced without waiting for an inbound shipment. Those are the days that decide whether a project opens on time.

## What to verify

Ask for the workshop address and check it against the cluster rather than taking a showroom address as proof. Then ask which processes are in-house: coating, weaving, tube bending, welding, upholstery.

In-house coating and weaving are the two that matter most for consistency, because they are the two that drift silently between batches. Anyone outsourcing both is a trader with a catalogue.

## Why it matters for reorders

The cluster advantage compounds. A factory inside it can hold your tooling, keep your colour file and produce spares long after the original order, which is what makes a range sellable in year three.

Ask for a live walk on a working day, and ask to see the staging warehouse where finished goods wait for loading. A full staging area is the most honest lead time indicator there is.
