# How to Audit a China Outdoor Furniture Factory Before Your First Order

Every week a buyer writes to us with the same story: they found a supplier, the price looked great, and then the container arrived with cracked welds and fading rattan. It didn't have to happen — a one-hour audit, done right, would have caught every problem.

This guide is the audit we wish every buyer ran before their first wholesale order. It's the same checklist we hold ourselves to at SOLAIREVA, an outdoor furniture manufacturer in Shunde, China.

---

## Before the audit: the documents to request

A genuine China outdoor furniture factory can produce these without hesitation. A trading company will stall.

- **Business license (营业执照)** — check the registered address matches the factory, and that the scope says "manufacturing," not just "import/export."
- **Export license** — confirms they actually ship goods overseas.
- **ISO 9001 certificate** — if they claim it, ask to see it.
- **Test reports** — UV stability, salt spray, load-bearing. These are the numbers that matter for outdoor furniture.
- **Customer references in your market** — e.g. hotel furniture suppliers in UAE, or furniture suppliers in Australia.

> **The one-question test:** *"Can I see your factory floor on a live video call this week?"* A factory says yes. A trader says "let me check."

---

## On-site: the 6 things to verify

### 1. The production line is actually running
A rented showroom with sample photos isn't a factory. Ask for a live video of the line mid-production — not a prepared tour. If they can't show you a running line, walk away.

### 2. Powder coating — in-house or outsourced?
Powder coated aluminum frames are the commercial standard. A factory that does its own coating line controls quality; one that outsources it adds a link in the chain that can fail.

### 3. Welding quality
Look at the welds — even beads, no spatter, no gaps. Cheap frames fail at the weld, not the tube. This is where most cost-cutting happens.

### 4. Rattan weaving density and UV additive
High-density PE rattan with a real UV additive package survives years outdoors. Cheap rattan fades and splits in one season. Ask what additive package they use — and ask for the UV test report (2,000+ hours), not the word "UV resistant."

### 5. Cushion foam density
Commercial-grade foam is 30-35 kg/m³ high-resilience. Standard residential foam sags after a season of daily use. The foam density spec is where a cheap quote always cuts corners.

### 6. Carton and container packing standard
Most freight damage happens here. Look at how they pack: carton wall strength, edge protectors, bracing inside the container. Ask to see photos of their last few container loads.

## The commercial checks

- **MOQ: per model or per container?** A mixed container of stock models is a normal entry order for real factories.
- **Payment terms:** 30% deposit / 70% against Bill of Lading is the fair standard. 100% up front is a red flag.
- **Defect rate:** a factory that measures it can quote it. We hold 0.47% across a 4-stage QC — incoming material, in-production, pre-packing, pre-shipping. If a supplier can't give you a number, they're not measuring it.
- **Third-party inspection:** will they accept SGS / Bureau Veritas / Intertek pre-shipment inspection? A real factory says yes.

---

## Red flags that end the audit

🚩 Can't give a real factory address
🚩 Avoids a live video of the floor
🚩 No test reports for UV / salt spray
🚩 Quotes far below every other factory for the same spec
🚩 Demands 100% payment up front
🚩 No customer references in your market
🚩 Won't put the spec sheet into the PO

Any one of these — walk away. There are hundreds of honest factories in Shunde and Foshan; you don't need to gamble on one that won't open its doors.
