# OEM vs ODM for Custom Outdoor Furniture: What Importers Need to Know

## OEM — your spec, their build

You provide the design or spec; the factory makes it. Common for importers who know exactly what they want: your sizes, fabric, frame, logo.

OEM usually needs a full container to justify new SKUs, packaging and admin. Ask what's free (colors, fabric, logo) vs what costs (new molds, frame redesign).

## ODM — they design and build

The factory brings a design and you customize it. Faster, lower risk, but you don't own the design unless the agreement says so.

For ODM, confirm the design ownership in writing — this is where importers get burned.

## Protect the process

Approve the pre-production sample in writing before mass production. That sample is your color, size and quality contract. Confirm how a mid-order spec change affects the ship date — the honest factories tell you the date impact.
