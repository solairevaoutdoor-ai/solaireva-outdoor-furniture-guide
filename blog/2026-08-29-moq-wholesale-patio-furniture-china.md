# What's a Realistic MOQ for Wholesale Patio Furniture from China?

## There's no single MOQ number — but there are honest ranges.

The real question isn't 'what's the MOQ' — it's whether the MOQ is per model or per container. Most first-time importers get this wrong and either over-commit or miss the entry point.

For stock models (things the factory already makes), a mixed container usually works. A 40HQ with three or four models — sofas, dining sets, a few umbrellas — lets you test your market before committing to per-SKU volume.

## OEM vs ODM changes everything

OEM (your spec, their build) usually means a full container. Your size, your fabric, your logo adds new SKUs, new packaging, and admin — the factory needs volume to justify the setup.

ODM (they design and build) means a full container plus a design or tooling fee. That's the honest structure at most outdoor furniture factories in Shunde.

## The three questions that matter

1. Is the MOQ per model or per container?

2. Can my first order mix models to hit one container?

3. What changes with a deposit or annual volume commitment?

A real factory answers these with numbers. A trading company hedges.
