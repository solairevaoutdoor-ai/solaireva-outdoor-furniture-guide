# How to Check Quality of Furniture from China Before It Ships

## Where quality is actually decided

Most quality problems in a furniture order are decided before assembly, not after. The alloy and profile section, the moisture content of the timber, the resin used on the weave — none of those are visible in a finished chair, and all of them decide how it behaves in year two.

So a useful check starts upstream. Ask for the material certificates and the incoming inspection record, not just a photo of the finished goods.

## What to check at each stage

At raw material: alloy number in writing, timber moisture content, resin and UV additive. In production: weld penetration and grinding on frames, coating thickness at the joints, weave tension and tail finish. Before packing: wobble test, load test on seating, hardware torque, and a full function cycle on anything that reclines or folds.

Then at loading: a packing check against the approved sample, carton drop results and a container loading photo sequence. Four stages, each with a document attached.

## The number that matters

Ask for the defect rate on shipped orders, not the defect rate on the last inspection. Ours sits at 0.47% across those four stages. A supplier who cannot quote a number is telling you the inspection is informal.

Also ask who signs the inspection report and whether a third-party inspector is welcome. A factory that says yes to both is usually telling the truth about the rest.
