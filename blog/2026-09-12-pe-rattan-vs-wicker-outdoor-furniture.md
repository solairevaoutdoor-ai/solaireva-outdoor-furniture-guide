# PE Rattan vs Natural Wicker: Which Lasts Longer Outdoors?

## The quick answer

For commercial outdoor use, high-density PE rattan with a UV additive package is the better choice. Natural wicker looks warmer but degrades outdoors — it's a covered-patio material.

## Why PE rattan wins outdoors

PE rattan is synthetic, so it doesn't absorb moisture, split, or mildew. With a real UV additive package, it holds color through years of full-sun exposure.

Natural wicker is woven from real materials — it weathers, splits, and fades. Indoors or under cover it's beautiful; outdoors it's a maintenance problem.

## What to ask the factory

Ask for the UV test hours and the additive package spec. 'UV resistant' without a report is a word, not a spec. A 2,000-3,000 hour test means the rattan will hold its color in commercial use.
