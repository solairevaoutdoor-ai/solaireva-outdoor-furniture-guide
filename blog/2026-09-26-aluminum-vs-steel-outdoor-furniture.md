# Aluminum vs Steel Outdoor Furniture: What to Spec for Commercial Use

## The short version

For commercial outdoor furniture — hotels, resorts, restaurants — aluminum wins on longevity, and here's why.

6063 aluminum with powder coating is the commercial standard. It's lighter, rust-resistant, and doesn't corrode at the weld points the way steel does.

## Where steel still makes sense

Steel is heavier and cheaper, and with a proper coating it works for covered applications. But near the coast, or in daily commercial use, steel frames corrode where the coating chips — and the welds go first.

If you're comparing quotes, ask for the salt spray test hours. A 500-hour salt spray result means one thing; 2,000 hours means another.

## What to write into the PO

Frame alloy (specify 6063), coating type and thickness, salt spray test hours, and weld finish. The PO is your contract — a spec sheet written into it is the only thing that protects you later.
