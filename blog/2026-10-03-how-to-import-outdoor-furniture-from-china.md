# How to Import Outdoor Furniture from China: A Step-by-Step for First-Time Buyers

## Step 1 — Find real factories, not just listings

Search platforms like Alibaba and Made-in-China, then verify. The outdoor furniture factories you want are clustered in Shunde and Foshan. A supplier that can't tell you which cluster it's in is a red flag.

## Step 2 — Verify the factory before paying

Ask for a live video call on the production floor, check the business license, ask for the UV test report. The one-question test: 'Can I see your factory floor on a live video call this week?' A factory says yes.

## Step 3 — Negotiate MOQ and payment

A mixed container of stock models is a fair entry order. Payment of 30% deposit / 70% against Bill of Lading is the standard that protects both sides.

## Step 4 — QC and inspection

Use third-party inspection (SGS, Bureau Veritas, Intertek) if you're not visiting. A factory that accepts inspection has nothing to hide.

## Step 5 — Container shipping

Plan the mixed load with the factory's measured loadable volume. Get FOB + freight quoted separately, and budget import duty and destination charges.
