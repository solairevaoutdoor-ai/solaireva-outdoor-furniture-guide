# Hotel Furniture Supplier China: What to Verify Before You Commit

## A hotel order is a repeat order

Hotel projects rarely buy once. Rooms open in phases, terraces get extended, and a manager replaces what a storm took. So the question for a hotel furniture supplier in China is not what they can ship in March — it is whether the November order matches it.

That comes down to records: a signed physical colour chip per material, gloss level, weave batch note, and the fabric dye lot. Photos of an approved sample are the most common cause of an off-shade second delivery.

## What the tender will ask for

Coastal and poolside hotel projects increasingly put 316 stainless hardware, solution-dyed acrylic and quick-dry foam in the base requirement rather than as an upgrade. Have the test documents ready: salt spray hours on the coating, UV hours on the weave, foam type and drain time.

Fire rating on cushions and foam is the other line that shows up late. If the supplier has never been asked for it, the project will find out in the wrong week.

## Spares and serviceable parts

Agree a serviceable parts list in writing — zip, cover, feet, fixing kit, foam insert — with a lead time against each. That is the document that gets used in year two, and it is the difference between a supplier and a vendor.

Ask for the spares lead time as a real number before you place phase one. Anyone can quote a first order; a hotel furniture supplier worth keeping quotes the second one.
