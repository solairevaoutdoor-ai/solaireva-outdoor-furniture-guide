# Import Duty on Furniture from China: USA, UK, EU and Australia

## The honest answer: it depends on the HTS code

Furniture isn't one product to customs — it's dozens of HTS codes. A patio chair, a dining table, an umbrella and a fire pit each classify differently, with different duty rates.

The best move is a licensed customs broker for your specific SKUs. But here's a planning frame.

## Rough duty ranges (2026 planning levels)

USA: outdoor furniture typically 3-6% (some teak items higher). UK: similar range under the UK Global Tariff. EU: 0-4% for most outdoor furniture. Australia: 5% for most, but check the tariff concession.

On top of duty, budget destination charges — terminal handling, customs clearance, inland haulage. These are often bigger than the duty itself.

## Landed cost, done right

FOB + freight + insurance + duty + destination charges = landed cost. Compare suppliers on landed cost, not FOB. The 'cheapest' quote is often the most expensive landed.

Ask the factory for FOB and honest packed dimensions so your freight forwarder can quote accurately.
