## 1. The 6 mistakes first-time buyers make

1. **Buying on price alone.** The cheapest quote usually means the thinnest frame, the cheapest coating, the rattan that fades in one season. A container that arrives with cracked welds costs more than the savings.
2. **Not checking whether they're a factory or a trading company.** A trading company can quote anything — it has no factory to hold accountable.
3. **Skipping the audit.** You wouldn't hire an employee without an interview. Don't pick a manufacturer without a factory check.
4. **Ignoring the spec sheet.** "UV resistant" is a word. A 3,000-hour UV test report is proof.
5. **Not photographing the container load.** If the cartons aren't braced correctly, the freight damage claim is on you.
6. **Paying 100% up front.** A genuine manufacturer will negotiate. 30% deposit / 70% against B/L is common and fair.

## 2. How to find outdoor furniture manufacturers in China

### Where buyers actually look

- **Alibaba / Made-in-China / Global Sources** — start here, but treat every listing as "a company that says they're a factory."
- **Trade shows** — Canton Fair (Guangzhou), CIFF (Shanghai). The big furniture factories in China all show at these.
- **Google search** — search *outdoor furniture manufacturer in China*, *wholesale patio furniture supplier*, *China furniture factory*.
- **Shunde (Foshan)** — the furniture capital of China. If you're sourcing outdoor furniture, Shunde is where the concentration of factories is.

> 💡 **Where the factories actually are:** Foshan/Shunde (outdoor + living room), Dongguan (furniture + home), Zhongshan (outdoor, lighting). A company that says it's a factory but can't tell you which cluster it's in is a red flag.

### A working shortlist process

1. Shortlist 5-8 companies from the platforms above.
2. Ask each for: **factory address, business license, real product photos (not catalog renders), and a video walkthrough.**
3. Cross-check the address on Google Maps — does it match what they claim?
4. Ask for **customer references in your market** (e.g. *hotel furniture suppliers in UAE* or *furniture suppliers in Australia*).
5. Shortlist to 2-3 for a deeper audit (Section 4).

## 3. Factory vs trading company — how to tell them apart

| Signal | Genuine factory | Trading company |
|--------|----------------|-----------------|
| Owns the production line | ✅ | ❌ |
| Shows workshop videos from its own plant | ✅ | Usually not |
| Can quote per-piece material costs | ✅ | ❌ |
| Factory address matches photos | ✅ | Sometimes generic |
| Business license shows "manufacturing" | ✅ | "Import/export" only |
| Will let you visit / video call the floor | ✅ | Hesitates |

**The one-question test:** *"Can I see your factory floor on a live video call this week?"* A factory says yes. A trader says "let me check."

## 4. The factory audit checklist (before you pay)

### Documents to request
- Business license (营业执照) — check the registered address and "manufacturing" scope
- Export license
- ISO 9001 (quality management) if claimed
- Test reports (UV, salt spray, load-bearing)

### What to verify on site or by video
- [ ] Production line is actually running (not a rented showroom)
- [ ] Powder coating line or outsourcing? (powder coated aluminum frames done in-house is a good sign)
- [ ] Welding quality — even beads, no spatter
- [ ] Rattan weaving density and UV additive mix
- [ ] Cushion foam density (commercial grade = higher density)
- [ ] **Carton and container packing standard** — this is where damage happens

### The checklist you should insist on
At SOLAIREVA we run a **4-stage QC** (incoming material, in-production, pre-packing, pre-shipping) and hold a **0.47% defect rate**. Ask any supplier for their numbers. If they can't give you a defect rate, they're not measuring it.

## 5. MOQ, pricing and payment terms explained

### Realistic MOQs for wholesale outdoor furniture from China
- **Stock models** (sofas, dining sets, loungers): often 1-2 containers or as low as a mixed container of standard models
- **Custom / OEM outdoor furniture**: higher — usually a full container or a quantity that justifies tooling and new SKUs
- Some factories (including us) do **mixed-container orders** — fill a 40HQ with multiple models

### Pricing reality
- Price = **material + labor + coating + packing + margin**. If a quote is far below every other quote for the same spec, they're cutting something — usually the coating or the rattan additive.
- Get the **spec sheet written into the PO** (frame wall thickness, coating, rattan type, foam density). The PO is your contract.

### Payment terms
- Standard: **30% deposit, 70% against Bill of Lading** (or before shipment).
- Avoid: 100% up front. Also be careful of "sample fee" scams that never ship samples.

## 6. Quality control: inspection, testing, and defect rates

### The tests that matter for outdoor furniture
| Test | What it proves |
|------|----------------|
| **UV stability test** | Rattan won't fade in 1-2 seasons. Look for 2,000-3,000 hours. |
| **Salt spray test** | Powder coating won't corrode near the coast. |
| **Load test** | The frame holds real weight — not just a showroom sit. |
| **Foam density test** | Cushions won't flatten after a season. |

### Third-party inspection
Use **SGS / Bureau Veritas / Intertek** for pre-shipment inspection if you're not visiting. It costs a few hundred USD and saves a container of headaches.

### What a good factory does at loading
Cartons braced, edge protectors, no over-stuffing, photos of every stage. If the factory photographs the container as it loads (we do), that's a sign they've been burned before — and learned.

## 7. Shipping: container types, freight, and import duty from China

### Container basics
- **20HQ**: ~28 m³ loadable — good for small first orders
- **40HQ**: ~68 m³ loadable — the workhorse for wholesale
- Mixing models in one container is standard practice from a real manufacturer

### Freight cost reality
Freight varies with season and port. Roughly, a 40HQ from South China to a major US/Europe port can run from a few thousand USD up, depending on peak season. **Get the freight quoted separately from the FOB price** — don't let it be hidden in a lump sum.

### Import duty from China
- **USA**: furniture import duty varies by HTS code — check with a customs broker
- **EU / UK**: similar — HTS classification matters
- **Australia / UAE / Kenya / South Africa**: each has its own schedule

Search *import duty on furniture from china to [your country]* and *shipping furniture from china to [your country]* — then confirm with a licensed customs broker, not just Google.

## 8. Materials: aluminum, PE rattan, teak — what to spec

### Aluminum frames
- **6063 aluminum alloy** + **powder coating** is the commercial standard.
- Ask: coating thickness, and whether it's been salt-spray tested.
- **Aluminum vs steel:** aluminum is lighter and rust-resistant; steel is heavier and cheaper but needs proper coating. For hotels and resorts, aluminum wins on longevity.

### PE rattan
- High-density PE rattan with a **UV additive package** is what survives years outdoors.
- Cheap rattan fades, cracks, and splits. The additive is where corners get cut.

### Teak
- **Grade A** teak is the commercial standard for premium outdoor dining.
- Ask for the grade in writing. "Teak" alone means nothing.

## 9. OEM/ODM and custom outdoor furniture

If you need **custom outdoor furniture** — your own sizes, colors, fabric, frame, or logo:

1. Confirm the factory does **OEM** (make to your spec) vs **ODM** (design + make).
2. Ask what **customization is free** (color, fabric, logo) vs what costs (new molds, frame redesign).
3. Get the **prototype / pre-production sample** approved in writing before mass production.
4. Confirm how a **mid-order spec change** affects the ship date — the honest factories tell you the date impact.

---

## 10. Red flags and how to walk away

🚩 **Walk away if a supplier:**
- Can't give you a real factory address
- Avoids a live video of the production floor
- Has no test reports for UV / salt spray
- Quotes a price far below every other factory for the same spec
- Demands 100% payment up front
- Has no customer references in your market
- Won't put the spec sheet into the PO
